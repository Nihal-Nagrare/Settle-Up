🧠 How the Greedy Minimum Cash-Flow Algorithm Works
O(N log N) Mathematical Proof

Instead of settling dozens of circular bilateral debts (e.g. Alice paying Bob, Bob paying Charlie, Charlie paying Alice), the algorithm nets each person's total paid vs consumed share into a single balance. It then greedily matches the person with the largest negative balance (maximum debtor) with the person with the largest positive balance (maximum creditor) until all balances reach exact zero.
